Today, building of small-scale space shuttle model (Space Shuttle Challenger) has started. Scale is 1:85, converted from meters to millimiters with preserving of two decimal places for specific wood cutting. More than two decimal places is redundant as the wood is highly porous and highly dependent on the conditions like moisture. Official, highly-detailed blueprints from NASA were used and probably slight modifications to be made, for example the angle at which body is tilted, which might as a result affect the flight. Nose-to-tail length is 438.12 mm. It is planned to add multiple engines, inspired from the real-world version, which will add a powerful thrust to the light wooden model.
