Wondering why does people know the past, but not the future? Main idea of the future is that we can't predict it. But why? Why this knowledge is not accessible to humans? Is knowing the future — where humanity is going to end up with — is against the nature? 

To vizualize this, one should imagine a physical process that happens in the nature. But the problem is that time is slightly different from the natural processes and simple analogies, like tidying a room, or heat flow from the coffee to the surroundings. It's more of an abstract dimension? It's a developed dimension by humans.  

There's no tomorrow, and yesterday at core — all we have things becoming more disorganized and chaotic. Then, why do we need time? Is it an attempt of humans to make their life more predictable? There was no time — humans developed such concept to decrease the chaos of the life. It's a formality. We used mathematics, physics and sciences to invent time.
