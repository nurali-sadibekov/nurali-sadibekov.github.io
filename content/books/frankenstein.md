## Comment

Shelley turns a story of scientific ambition into a question of responsibility: what does a creator owe to what they bring into the world? The novel's layered narration also makes it difficult to reduce its characters to simple heroes and villains.

## A quote

> Beware, for I am fearless and therefore powerful.

— Mary Shelley, *Frankenstein*, Chapter 20
