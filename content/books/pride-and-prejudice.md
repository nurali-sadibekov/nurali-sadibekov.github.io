## Comment

Austen makes the conventions of courtship and class both funny and revealing. Elizabeth's sharp observations give the novel its energy, while her changing view of Darcy makes it a story about learning to question first impressions.

## A quote

> It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune must be in want of a wife.

— Jane Austen, *Pride and Prejudice*, Chapter 1
