What I value about engineering is that it's an independent view on life itself. It's an independent angle. From which, you can observe nature differently. Taking any natural phenomena - you have  an understanding not only of the physics behind itself, but, in my case, understanding that there's something deeper than the first impression. There's a reason for something happening. Nothing happens without a reason. 

Even though I might not work as an engineer, it will still be my background. Part of my development. If I will not be an engineer, I will be someone who values it and is not blind to it. During the Bachelor program, I had a chance to eliminate my initial thought on engineering. It was something looking easy at first, until you understood that it's a huge field with lots of variables. That's the importance and value of having an engineering degree, at least for me. 

I am not an A student, most of the engineering subjects are challenging for me. But it still has value when you learn part of it. Just like a book - you might not fully understand the meaning behind it, but it will leave a mark on you.
